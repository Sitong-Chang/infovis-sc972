Baseline

Additional data

Three consecutive records from Midjourney Threads, thread 2231, preserve exact prompts, timestamps, record IDs, and upscale labels.

Emerging technology

New prompt-conditioned images and pre-generated multimodal summaries are visibly labeled and separated from recorded evidence.
